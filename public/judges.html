Morning Desk, testing guide

Five minutes, seated, no controller.

Before you start

  1. Sit at a desk or table in a room the headset already knows (its own room setup is what lets apps see your table and walls).
  2. On Meta Quest, open the Browser and go to the Morning Desk page.
  3. Pinch Enter Morning Desk. If the headset asks to use your room's layout, allow it.

On a computer, the same page opens a built-in Quest 3 emulator with a synthetic office. Click Enter Morning Desk and use the emulator's on-screen controls to turn the headset and move the hands.

The 60-second path

  1. Watch the brief land: tiles flat on your desk, the main chart on the wall ahead, today's tasks on the side wall.
  2. Pinch the Deposits tile. Its detail card stands up behind it.
  3. Rest your hands. Look at the Withdrawals tile and hold still until the ring fills.
  4. Look at a bar on the chart, then pinch Why did this drop? on the question card at the right of your desk. The bar lights up and the answer names the categories that moved it.
  5. Pinch Pin a note here. The answer is pinned to the wall above the chart.

Things worth trying

  • Hands-free: every tile, bar, card button and setting also works by looking at it and holding still. Card buttons ask for a two-second hold so a passing glance never asks a question.
  • Compare with last week draws the same days one week earlier as a dashed line. Show only defense spending filters the chart.
  • Settings, at the left of your desk: high contrast, captions, one-handed mode, sound, and lay out again.
  • Frame time: pinch the clock tile to show the frame-time card.
  • A different room: in a room with no table in reach, the tiles move to a tray at lap height; with no wall in reach, the chart curves around you at 1.2 meters. In the emulator, the environment button on the top toolbar switches rooms.
  • Come back tomorrow: reopen the page. Panels return to the surfaces you left them on, pinned notes stay, the tiles show what changed since your last visit, and the clock tile counts your mornings in a row.
  • Your own data: on the start page, open Make it yours to set today's tasks or chart any public CSV with date and value columns.
  • Voice: where the browser offers speech recognition, an Ask out loud line appears on the question card.

The data

Morning Desk runs on the U.S. Treasury's Daily Treasury Statement (fiscaldata.treasury.gov), public data published every business day. When the live data cannot be reached, a saved copy loads instead and the chart says so. No account, no sign-in and no personal data are needed.
